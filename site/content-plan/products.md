## The Collection

Every piece is made to order for your wedding. Nothing is kept in stock, nothing is made twice the same way. Please allow a lead time of four to six weeks from design approval, and enquire early for peak season dates.

All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Delivery is quoted separately.

---

### 1. The Duet: Hand-Painted Bonbon Duo Box

Two bonbons, each shell hand-painted in swirls of gold and bronze cocoa butter, resting in a matte black box with a silver-foiled lid. Choose two fillings from our signature range, from salted caramel to native Davidson plum ganache.

**$14.50 per box. Minimum order 50 boxes.**

---

### 2. The Medallion: 24ct Gold Leaf Chocolate Medallion

A single medallion of 70% dark couverture, embossed with your monogram or wedding motif and finished with a touch of edible 24ct gold leaf. Presented in a black gift sleeve tied with silk ribbon.

**$12 per piece. Minimum order 60 pieces.**

---

### 3. The One: Black Velvet Single Truffle Box

One perfect truffle, hand-rolled and dusted, seated in a black velvet-touch box with a magnetic closure. Quiet on the outside, remarkable on the inside. Available in dark, milk or single-origin ganache.

**$11 per box. Minimum order 50 boxes.**

---

### 4. The Signature: Monogram Bar

A slim 45g bar of couverture chocolate cast with your joined initials and wedding date, wrapped in gold-flecked paper with a black wax seal. Choose dark, milk or caramelised white chocolate.

**$16 per bar. Minimum order 40 bars.**

---

### 5. The Trio: Silver-Dusted Praline Trio

Three pralines in graduating shades, each finished with a whisper of edible silver lustre, set in a beige linen-textured box with a silver clasp. Hazelnut, almond and pistachio praline, or a trio designed around your menu.

**$19.50 per box. Minimum order 50 boxes.**

---

### 6. The Sculpture: Bespoke Commission Piece

A one-off sculptural piece in chocolate, designed with you: a pair of entwined rings, a flower from your bouquet, a form that belongs to your story. Cast, carved and finished by hand as a centrepiece or a gift between the two of you.

**From $320 per commission. Price on design.**
